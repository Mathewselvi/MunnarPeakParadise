Welcome to Munnar Peak Paradise. By making a reservation with us, you agree to the following terms and conditions:

  • All reservations must be guaranteed with a valid credit card or advance payment
  • Room rates are subject to applicable taxes and service charges
  • Check-in time is 2:00 PM and check-out time is 11:00 AM

  • Valid photo identification is required at check-in

2. Payment Policies

Our payment terms are designed to ensure a smooth booking experience:

  • A deposit of 30% is required at the time of booking
  • Full payment is due upon check-in unless other arrangements have been made
  • We accept cash, major credit cards, and digital payment methods
  • All payments are processed in Indian Rupees (INR)
  • Additional charges for services, meals, and amenities will be added to your final bill

3. Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans can change. Our cancellation policy is as follows:

  • Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in date
  • Cancellations within 48 hours will incur a charge equivalent to one night's stay
  • No-shows will be charged the full reservation amount
  • Peak season and holiday bookings may have different cancellation terms
  • Group bookings (5+ rooms) require special cancellation arrangements

Important Notice

During peak season (December-January and April-May), advance booking is highly recommended. Special events and festivals may require longer advance notice and different terms.
